But the largest finite magnitude E2M1 can represent is 3.0 (nibble 0x5/0xD, `biased_e=2, m=1`); `encode_fp4_e2m1` saturates any value with `biased_e >= 3` (i.e., magnitude >= 4.0) to ±Inf. This wastes roughly one exponent level of dynamic range for the rest of the channel's weights and injects a guaranteed Inf into every quantized channel's largest-magnitude weight.
